About

Engineering for the web.
Thinking about growth.

Hello! I am Md. Mayen, a dedicated Full-Stack Software Developer and Meta Ads Campaign Specialist based in Bangladesh. I specialize in crafting robust, high-performing web applications and driving measurable business growth through targeted performance marketing.

My core expertise lies in the modern JavaScript ecosystem—building responsive user interfaces with React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS, alongside developing solid backend systems using Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB. Beyond writing clean code, I combine technical engineering with direct-to-consumer (D2C) marketing strategies to build scalable digital experiences.

Currently, I am leveraging my full-stack capabilities and performance marketing insights to architect two personal e-commerce platforms from the ground up:

  1. Globoix: A wholesale-to-consumer e-commerce initiative engineered to streamline B2B-to-C sales channels and deliver high-value products to retail customers.
  2. Chaser BD: A modern fashion e-commerce brand designed with seamless shopping flows, custom storefront design, and integrated performance marketing funnels.

Whether architecting end-to-end full-stack software solutions or optimizing high-converting Meta ad channels, I am driven by technical precision and business growth. I am open to technical collaborations, full-stack projects, and client inquiries.
